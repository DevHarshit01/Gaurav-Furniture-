About Us

Crafting Beautiful Spaces Since 1994

Gaurav Furniture in Govind Nagar, Kanpur is counted among the most trusted names in furniture retail — a premier Nilkamal dealer offering sofas, beds, dining sets, wardrobes, office furniture, steel & wooden furniture, and premium home décor.

Located at Furniture Market, near Sangeet Talkies and Hero Ganj, our showroom is easy to reach with multiple transport options. Our experienced team delivers excellent service and helps you find the perfect furniture for your home or office.
